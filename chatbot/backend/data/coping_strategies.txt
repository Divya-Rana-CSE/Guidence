Here are a few coping strategies you can try when feeling overwhelmed:

1. Take a short walk in nature to ground yourself.
2. Try the 5-4-3-2-1 grounding exercise using your senses.
3. Write down your thoughts in a journal to process your emotions.
4. Practice slow, deep breathing for 2–5 minutes.
5. Reach out to a friend or write a message you may or may not send.
6. Listen to a calming playlist or ambient sound.
7. Remind yourself: “This moment will pass. I’ve survived 100% of my hardest days so far.”
